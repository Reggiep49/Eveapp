# Eve

The seventh edition replaces the previous dashboard and navigation with a Christmas ritual: a living snow globe, a keepsake book, wishes tucked into photographs, a letter sealed in wax, and a cinematic flight to the North Pole.

Parent curation, the received letter, checkout, and delivery tracking live in focused panels around the same world. Existing local demo data is preserved under the original storage keys. All assets are served locally. There are no retailer, email, payment, affiliate, analytics, microphone, or push connections.

## Validation

- JavaScript syntax checks: eve.js, model.js, holiday.js.
- Desktop browser: store and gift selection, excluded-toy masking, child personalization, page turns, wish add/remove, 3D rotation and zoom, sample dictation, letter submission and timed finale.
- Parent letter: five wishes total $189.95; removing the $44.99 truck gives $144.96. Demo card and order flow verified.
- Receipt scan and CSV download, tracking extraction, shipped and delayed events, adjusted arrival dates, delayed filter and package history verified.
- Phone-size 390 × 844 preview: home, single-page book, toy dialog, and parent curation inspected.
- Production access remains owner-only. Payment, email parsing, and notifications remain explicitly simulated.

The 3D toys are stylized interactive models, not retailer scans. This static prototype saves preferences, wishes, and sample orders in the visitor's browser.

## Visual assets

The new snow globe was generated for this edition: cinematic macro photography of an heirloom crystal snow globe, walnut and brass base, snowy alpine village, gold observatory, train and lanterns beneath a winter aurora; dark evergreen palette, warm illuminated windows, tactile materials, no text or UI. Existing illustrated chapter spreads and North Pole flight artwork remain in use.
